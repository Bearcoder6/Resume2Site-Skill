# Information Architecture Rules

Choose the page structure from the resume's real content, not from a fixed template.

## First Decide The Content Center

- `academic`: publications, research interests, advisors, labs, academic awards, education, and research projects lead.
- `engineering-job`: role positioning, selected projects, internships, systems/stack skills, and GitHub/demo links lead.
- `business`: role scope, outcomes, experience, capability areas, and public work/case links lead.
- `creative`: visual work, case studies, process, client-safe outcomes, and portfolio links lead.
- `minimal`: identity, concise experience, education, skills, and contact lead.

Record the selected content center in `work/site-plan.md`.

## Page Priority

The first viewport should contain only:

- name
- role or academic positioning
- one short source-grounded bio
- key public links
- portrait when available
- optional person-level or education-level facts

Keep detailed project, internship, paper, and award facts in their own sections.

## Global Positioning

- Write global positioning from repeated evidence across education, experience, projects, papers, and user goals.
- Do not describe the website generation process, source resume, or test/demo purpose in public copy.
- Do not promote a single project into the hero or positioning paragraph unless it is clearly the user's primary identity or requested focus.
- If a project is relevant but not identity-defining, keep it in the projects section and use broader role language in the hero.

## Section Ordering

- Academic pages: profile, about, research interests, publications/papers, projects, education, honors, contact.
- Engineering pages: hero, selected projects, internship/experience, skills, education, awards, contact.
- Business pages: hero, capabilities, experience, selected outcomes, education/certifications, contact.
- Creative pages: hero, selected work gallery, case studies, process/services, experience, contact.
- Minimal pages: hero, experience/projects, education, skills, links/contact.

Omit empty sections. Do not create filler sections just to balance layout.

## Evidence Placement

- Personal/profile areas are for identity and public links.
- Project cards are for project-specific problems, methods, metrics, stacks, and links.
- Experience sections are for company/role-specific responsibilities and impact.
- Education sections are for GPA, rank, coursework, school roles, and degree information.
- Award sections are for competition details and prize levels.
