# Site Plan

Mode: academic
Style variant: academic-editorial
Audience: faculty, research collaborators, graduate admissions reviewers
Primary goal: present a credible academic profile with research interests, publications, projects, and honors.
Assumptions: no phone number should be shown publicly.

First viewport: profile card with name, headline, email, website, Scholar link, and research interests.
Section order: About, Research Interests, Publications, Projects, Education, Honors, Contact.
Content rewrite plan: keep publications factual and turn projects into short research summaries.
Visual direction: restrained editorial academic layout with subtle borders and near-white background.
Asset needs: optional CSS-only paper grid or subtle library/campus image if licensed.
Privacy decisions: show email and public academic links only.
Open questions: none.
