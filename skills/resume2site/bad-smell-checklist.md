# Bad Smell Checklist

Use this list during final review. If any item appears in generated files, fix it before reporting completion.

## Content Smells

- Project or experience numbers appear in personal/profile/hero/global metric areas.
- Phone number is public without user approval.
- Public links from the resume are missing without a note.
- The page exposes internal style variant names.
- The page exposes generation process, source file type, extraction workflow, privacy-rule decisions, final-review notes, or internal Skill behavior.
- The hero or positioning section is about the generator, the demo, the resume source, or one incidental project instead of the person.
- Public-facing sections use internal QA/demo wording such as test material, generated example, sample output, or automatic test.
- Public copy makes unsupported personality, motivation, or preference claims instead of source-grounded factual synthesis.
- The page uses English-heavy labels for a Chinese resume without user request.
- Resume bullets are pasted without rewriting into web-friendly sections.
- Claims are stronger than the resume supports.

## Design Smells

- Too many rounded cards or nested cards.
- Generic AI gradients, random glowing blobs, or glassmorphism everywhere.
- Hero title is oversized relative to the page.
- Section headings are visually louder than the person's name.
- Default browser typography or default-looking colors.
- Decorative metric cards exist only to fill space.
- Layout is too narrow and resume-like when desktop width is available.

## Asset Smells

- Portrait is missing even though the resume contains one.
- Portrait crops off the head or important content.
- Background image is blurry, low resolution, unrelated, or uncredited.
- Link icons are mismatched, unofficial, or visually inconsistent.
