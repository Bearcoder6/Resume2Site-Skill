# Academic Style Research

These are pre-distilled academic homepage style rules for the Skill. Apply them by default when generating academic mode.

## Common Strong Patterns

- Clear profile identity in the first viewport.
- Profile card or sidebar with photo, affiliation, contact, and links.
- About section that explains research area in plain language.
- Publications formatted with consistent title, authors, venue, year, and links.
- Minimal but careful typography.
- Near-white background with subtle borders.
- Compact news or updates section when the user has recent academic activity.

## How To Make It Look Good

- Use the profile card as an anchor, not a decorative block.
- Let publications create visual rhythm through title, metadata, venue, and links.
- Use restrained typography with strong section hierarchy.
- Keep colors quiet: ink, off-white, muted blue, muted green, warm gray.
- Use one subtle background device: paper grain, fine grid, campus/library image band, or low-contrast research texture.
- Keep About short and credible; avoid inspirational slogans.
- Make academic links compact and useful: PDF, code, project, DOI, arXiv, Scholar.

## Academic Visual Tone

- Restrained.
- Trustworthy.
- Readable.
- Source-grounded.
- Lightly editorial rather than commercial.

## Avoid

- Startup-style hero claims.
- Fake numbers or citation metrics.
- Huge gradients.
- Icon-heavy publication cards.
- Empty News sections.
- Overly cute illustrations.
